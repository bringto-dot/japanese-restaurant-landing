The counter

Two things, done properly.

We opened with a short menu on purpose. Octopus from the grill and a highball poured slowly, over ice cut by hand. Everything else on the board exists to sit beside those two.

There is no dining room. You sit at the counter, the grill is an arm's length away, and the last thing that happens to your plate happens in front of you.

Three from the chef

What he sends out first.

Three plates the chef will put in front of you before you have decided anything. The rest of the board changes with what came in that morning.

    1 200 ₽

    Salmon and cream cheese wrapped in mango, dressed leaves, lemon.

  2. Salmon Rose

Full menu at the counter. Set menu for six or more, arranged in advance.

The other half of the name

Whisky, soda, and patience.

Ice cut by hand at four, glass chilled, soda poured down the bar spoon so it keeps its edge. One stir. Never two.

  • Toki Highball 750 ₽

Chef

Pavel Sokolov

Six years behind counters in Osaka and Tokyo, then a narrow room off the Garden Ring. He still works the grill himself, every service.
